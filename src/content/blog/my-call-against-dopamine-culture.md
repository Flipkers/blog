Though algorithms help u discover new content, they actually have bigger mission to accomplish: increase your screen time in the app, which is destructive for your life. They have much no other choice, but control over you, free user. What u gonna do, complain? U accepted terms of use when u joined.

To avoid this trap, builders must make products with paid plan only, and u gotta pay for each of them. That’s the ideal scenario, where they have no incentive to increase ur screen time, you won’t see more ads, uve already paid. Thats why instagram dropped plans to launch no ads subscription: revenue from the plan cannot be compared with billions on ads.

That’s why I think the most popular services aren’t ur friends. And u shouldn’t befriend them too. Those who supposed to go against the trend, joined the “dark side” too. Look at substack. Folks grew on newsletters outside the algorithm, which people were read through Yahoo and Gmail… Then they launched an app with the algorithmic feed, reels, and stole subs from writers. I love capitalism.

Only one way to evade the trap: just stop consuming. Don’t consume anything in short format.

Read long essays, 15min articles, books. Watch 3 hours interviews on YT. You’ll feel awfully bored, and realize how addicted u were to the phone. My screen time on Iphone dropped from 5 hours to 1,5 h.

I believe in these tips, cuz I use them myself.
