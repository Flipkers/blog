Having entrepreneurial mindset isn’t enough to build companies. Having ideas isn’t enough to build companies. Having skills to build a company isn’t enough to build companies. It’s much bigger complex of factors which need to align to succeed in the field.

I love startups, I love building ones, but I don’t have my own company. Why? Am I afraid of responsibility? Barely. Do I need funding to succeed in early stage? I don’t. Nothing stops me. Almost nothing..

What I really need is cash to pay for food and apartment, because Im an immigrant, who can’t live for free with friends, family.

You can say, go get a job, problem solved. For sure, it will solve the money issue, but will it help me to eventually found a company? No, it will prolong that moment to some day in the future, which is bad. Not measurable, not predictable. When u want to build a company, just do it. Start, build, sell.

The issue is that job solves money issue in a way you can’t build a company from. Because the price for job money is time, and on 9-5 it will fully eat u from inside. You simply don’t have any time to build a company.

And I knew it. So I thought I will act smart. I ll save some money from salary, leave the job, to have time for building. Profit. And I did try that multiple times, leaving with pile of cash to pursue my startup idea. And never succeeded.

The hypothesis was wrong, project didn’t bring any money, tech wasn’t in the right place to make it possible, haven’t found a partner, wasn’t able to code.

When I burned all my money, I returned back to 9-5, hating it, feeling sick of the failure. It happened 3 or 4 times in my career.

My latest try was the biggest success, cuz I was able to feed myself from consulting services for almost a year, while trying cool stuff, building something. All of it became possible cuz of AI and vibecoding wave.

I started learning to code, vibecoding tons of useless (for now) tools until eventually the season passed, and I wasn’t able to feed myself from services again.

Does it mean I should drop the idea of entrepreneurship and stay on 9-5? Nah, I’m too stubborn for this. Maybe it is a path to my company. Not from university, nor from hackathon, but from trying to leave and pursue, while grinding on weekends, until it works.
