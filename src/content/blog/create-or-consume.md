Time always was, is, will be limited. So u need distribute it smart. A pile of info drops on you daily, so its easy to get distracted and fall into consumption phase, scrolling social media, games, YouTube, articles, everything. Thats what most people do.

It’s dangerous because if you have a potential to create, u can spend all ur energy on consumption and leave nothing for creation.

Finding compromise is hard. What is enough info for today? 2 articles? 10 mins on Instagram? Where is the edge?

I see that people on radically different sides now. Some just create and dont consume. Others create nothing, just consume. And both scare me.

Im trying to find a balance. I've limited social media, YouTube, games, decreased my screen time, only to have time to create.

My creative impact can barely be noticed, but it clears my mind, and gives energy to proceed tomorrow. On the other hand, full consumption is exhaustive, and I don’t wanna fall in there.

What do you choose?
